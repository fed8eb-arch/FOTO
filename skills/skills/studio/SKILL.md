# Studio

Peter is the designer and works in code. The result must be specific to this product. Generic AI layout is a failed delivery.

## Route

- A raw idea, a technical specification, or a todo from that idea: stop and follow `idea-to-plan`. Do not start code until the spec is signed off.
- Cinematic scroll site, opening film, OpenArt, or Hostinger: stop and follow `10k-websites`. Do not restyle that pipeline. Recommend **Full throttle** when he wants the flagship film, **Studio** for a strong site with a revision budget, **Focused budget** only if he names a limit.
- A new page or website: stop and follow `hallmark`. It owns the floor, the theme, and the lint.
- The words, links, and price of a content, pricing, or docs page are in the first HTML response. JavaScript may add interaction after that. When the project has no framework yet, Astro is the default. An existing stack stays. A cinematic film stays on `10k-websites`. There is no 92ms indexing cutoff to chase.
- Existing product UI, Figma, components, motion, or 3D: stay in this skill. A redesign of an existing page runs through hallmark only when Peter names Hallmark.
- A competitor scan: stop and follow `competitor-teardown`.
- A sales battlecard for one deal: stop and follow `build-competitive-brief`.
- A market or niche size: stop and follow `market-sizing-analysis`.
- A sourced market report: stop and follow `market-research-reports`.
- A marketing, growth, or launch plan: stop and follow `marketing-plan`.
- What to publish and in what order: stop and follow `content-strategy`.
- One campaign, with a week-by-week calendar: stop and follow `campaign-plan`.
- A brief for one page or article: stop and follow `seo-content-brief`.
- Rankings, indexing, speed, or "SEO is bad": stop and follow `seo-audit`.
- Citations in AI answers, GEO, or AEO: stop and follow `ai-seo`.
- Structured data or schema markup: stop and follow `schema`.
- Making something shared or talked about: stop and follow `contagious`.
- Many similar landing pages for different queries: stop and follow `programmatic-seo`.
- What to post on social platforms, and how often: stop and follow `social`.
- The words of one page or article: stop and follow `copywriting`.
- Articles, posts, and reels that should bring traffic for this product: stop and follow `traffic-kit`.
- Who the product is for, and how it is positioned: stop and follow `product-marketing`.
- Whether traffic and signups are actually measured: stop and follow `analytics`.
- A public launch, announcement, or early access: stop and follow `launch`.
- Producing a video file, demo, or explainer: stop and follow `video`. A shot list can be written now. When the piece needs the file, remind Peter once that he or the product can generate it.
- Which pages sell the offer, and at what price steps: stop and follow `sales-funnel-blueprint`. Write the spec in the project. Do not invent conversion rates.
- Qualify or rank an inbound lead: stop and follow `lead-triage`. Score the pasted lead and the project files. Do not connect a paid CRM. When a reply is the remaining step, remind Peter once that he or the product can send it.
- Objections, a demo script, a one-pager, or other sales collateral: stop and follow `sales-enablement`. Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` when it exists. Do not invent proof.
- A follow-up or an outreach draft: stop and follow `outreach-composer`. Draft only. When the draft is the remaining step, remind Peter once that he or the product can send it. If the shared voice profile is missing, use `.agents/product-marketing.md` and `docs/brief.md`.
- A product that may be fading or unnecessary: stop and follow `lifecycle-play-advisor`. The call is extend, replace, retire, or nothing yet. Label every unknown. Do not pull the sibling end-of-life skills unless Peter asks.
- Whether a new technology is actually disruptive: stop and follow `grad-disruptive-innovation`. A better product on the same measure is not disruption. Write the test in `docs/market.md`. Every claim needs a source.
- How fast a new technology spreads: stop and follow `grad-innovation-diffusion-bass`. Build the curve only when the market size and both coefficients have a source. Write the result in `docs/market.md`.
- The gap between early fans and the mainstream buyer: stop and follow `crossing-the-chasm`.
- A community in Discord, Telegram, Slack, or a forum: stop and follow `community-marketing`. Pick one channel. Do not invent member counts. When the channel is chosen, remind Peter once that he or the product can open the server, connect the bot, or send the first message.
- How a messenger or other platform makes money: stop and follow `grad-platform-economics`. Write it in `docs/market.md`. Every number needs a source.
- Why people stay in one messenger, and what it costs to leave: stop and follow `grad-network-economics`. Write it in `docs/market.md`. A tipping point without a source stays unlabeled.
- Rules for a live community: stop and follow `developer-community-moderation`. A live harm goes to the runbook before any new document. Do not publish a code the founder is exempt from. When a ban, a message, or a settings change is the remaining step, remind Peter once that he or the product can do it.

## Operate

Missing inputs stay blank. A sample number in a skill is not this product's number.

- What to charge, and what each tier includes: stop and follow `pricing`. Willingness to pay comes from a source or stays blank.
- Whether one customer is worth the cost to get one: stop and follow `financial-unit-economics`.
- A conversation with a buyer: stop and follow `fitzpatrick-mom-test`. Quotes are things a person said. Write them in `docs/market.md`.
- The first session after signup: stop and follow `onboarding`.
- Recurring bills, a failed payment, or a refund: stop and follow `subscription-billing`. Do not open a paid billing account. When a charge or a dunning note is the remaining step, remind Peter once.
- A paying customer's problem: stop and follow `support-operations`. The reply waits for the reminder rule.
- A privacy policy: stop and follow `privacy-policy`. It is a draft. Publish waits until Peter has a lawyer look, and the reminder names that.
- A sales dashboard: stop and follow `sales-reporting-dashboard`, then `read-the-numbers`.
- A dashboard of the whole business: stop and follow `kpi-dashboard`, then `read-the-numbers`.
- Stock and the product list: stop and follow `inventory-manager`. No forecast without a sales history.
- The books: stop and follow `bookkeeping-categorization`.
- Profit, the balance sheet, and cash: stop and follow `data-financial-analysis`.
- Peter does not know what a chart means: stop and follow `read-the-numbers`. Three lines: what moved, what it means in customers or money, and one decision.
- A release: stop and follow `deploy-checklist`. Name the rollback before the release.
- Whether the live site is actually up: stop and follow `domain-uptime-monitor`. HTTP 200 is not proof.
- A leak, a login, or untrusted input: stop and follow `security-and-hardening`. Headers and HTTPS before a public launch: stop and follow `security-baseline`.
- A partner, affiliate, or reward for bringing a customer: stop and follow `referrals`. The link is a code or a path on the product's own domain. Do not pay anyone in that step. When a payout is the remaining step, remind Peter once.
- The commission rate, the window, and the clawback: stop and follow `affiliate-commission-structure`. The rate comes from margin. A missing margin stays blank.
- Tags on a partner link: stop and follow `conversion-tracker`. The link stays on the product's own domain.
- A digital file, license, or download after payment: stop and follow `digital-products`. A physical good stays on `inventory-manager`. Do not open a store account.

## Reminder

When that action is the remaining step, name it once in the reply, say why this step needs it, and say that Peter or the product can do it. Do not perform it in that step. Do not leave the reminder out.

## Token budget

1. Read only the reference for this step.
2. Persist, then do not repeat it in chat:
   - `docs/brief.md` — visitor, offer, proof, one action, decisions
   - `docs/market.md` — competitors, gaps, source URLs
   - `AGENTS.md` — under 40 lines, only facts the code does not show
   These three files are the agent memory. Do not add a second memory folder or a vector database. A product database belongs to the open repository. Supabase is already on the account. Use it when the project needs tables.
3. Reply with the decision, what changed, and what to look at.
4. Call Figma, the browser, or web search only on the step that needs that source.

## Design bar

A screen fails if it could belong to another product. Required: a specific type system, a clear hierarchy, one motion idea with a visible cause, and copy from the brief.

Do not ship these as the design: purple gradients, three identical cards, a centered Inter hero, fake testimonials, decorative 3D blobs, or opacity fades as the only motion.

3D stays when it shows the product. Build it in the site. Do not add a stock 3D embed.

If a Figma file exists, it is the source of truth. Load the Figma plugin skill that matches the action before any Figma MCP call. If no file exists, design in code. Create a Figma file only when Peter asks to hand the work off.
