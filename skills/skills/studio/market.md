# Market research

Write `docs/market.md` in this shape. Keep each competitor to four lines.

```markdown
# Market
Visitor job:
Our offer:
One action we want:

## Competitors
### Name — url
- Who it is for:
- What they lead with:
- Proof they actually show:
- Gap we can own:

## Decision
Positioning:
What we will not copy:
Open questions:
```

## How to gather it

1. Ask Peter for the product and the visitor in one question if the brief does not say. Do not interview him field by field.
2. Search for the category and the phrases a buyer would type. Open the real sites. Use the browser already in Cursor.
3. Record only what is on the page: headline, audience, price if shown, proof, and the primary action. Quote short phrases and keep the URL.
4. Stop at 5 competitors unless the category is obviously broader. More pages cost more than they teach.
5. State the gap as a job the visitor still cannot finish. Do not invent awards, revenue, quotes, or review scores.

## What not to connect

Web search and the built-in browser cover this. Do not add another search or scraping MCP for a study. A notes database is optional and stays disconnected until Peter asks for one.
